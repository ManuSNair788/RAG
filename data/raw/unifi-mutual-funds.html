Unifi Asset Management Pvt Ltd is the mutual fund arm of Unifi Capital, an investment firm founded in 2001 providing portfolio management services (PMS) and alternative investment funds (AIF). 

The fund house received in-principle approval from the Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) in November 2024 to commence mutual fund operations. Unifi Mutual Fund’s philosophy is rooted in bottom-up investing and long-term value creation, i.e., to beat the market. 

Backed by an experienced investment team and a research-led process refined over years by managing millions of investor capital, Unifi Mutual Fund aims to offer focused, transparent, and thoughtfully constructed schemes across equity, hybrid, and debt categories.
